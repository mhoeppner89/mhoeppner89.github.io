Behavioral economics · AI · risk & uncertainty

Research on decisions under risk and uncertainty.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in behavioral economics at Brandenburg University of Technology Cottbus-Senftenberg. My research examines how people and adaptive or AI systems make decisions under uncertainty and navigate institutional constraints. I am particularly interested in how risk, information, delegation, and changing environments shape behavior and decision quality. I also use(d) to fence a lot. I swear my slop is human-supervised.
